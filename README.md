# customer-trends-data-analysis-SQL-Python-PowerBI
Complete Data Analytics Portfolio Project with end-to-end industry standard Data Analysis of Customer Shopping Trends from Retail Data using SQL, Python and Power BI.
Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis
An end-to-end data analytics solution—tracking spending patterns, customer segments, and product performance using Python, SQL, and Power BI.

1.  Purpose
The Customer Shopping Behavior Dashboard is a comprehensive analytics tool designed to uncover insights into consumer habits across 3,900 transactions. It provides stakeholders with actionable data on revenue drivers, subscription impact, and demographic preferences to guide strategic marketing and inventory decisions.

2.  Tech Stack

Python (pandas) – Used for data loading, cleaning, and initial exploratory data analysis (EDA).
PostgreSQL – Utilized for structured data storage and complex business logic queries.
Power BI – The primary visualization platform used to create the interactive Customer Behavior Dashboard.
Feature Engineering – Created new metrics like age_group and purchase_frequency_days to enhance segmentation.

3.  Dataset Summary
The dataset contains 3,900 rows and 18 columns covering various facets of the retail experience:
Demographics: Age, Gender, Location, and Subscription Status.
Transaction Details: Item Purchased, Category (Clothing, Accessories, etc.), Purchase Amount (USD), and Season.
Behavioral Data: Review Ratings, Shipping Type, Discount/Promo Code usage, and Frequency of Purchases.


4.Key Business Insights & Highlights
       
  1. Revenue & Demographics
             Gender Performance: Male customers contributed significantly higher revenue ($157,890) compared to Female customers ($75,191).
             Age Groups: "Young Adults" are the highest revenue contributors ($62,143), followed closely by "Middle-aged" shoppers.
             Geographic Top Performer: Montana was identified as a top location for frequency.

 2. Subscription & Loyalty
            Subscription Split: 27% of customers are subscribers, while 73% are not.
            Spending Habits: Interestingly, non-subscribers have a slightly higher average spend ($59.87) compared to subscribers ($59.49).
            Segmentation: The majority of the customer base is classified as "Loyal" (3,116 customers), with a smaller segment of "Returning" and "New" shoppers.

3. Product & Shipping Trends
           Top Products: Clothing is the most frequent category, with Blouses and Jewelry being top-selling items.
           Highest Rated: Gloves and Sandals lead in customer satisfaction with average ratings above 3.84.
           Shipping Impact: Express shipping users have a higher average purchase amount ($60.48) than standard shipping users ($58.46).
           Discount Dependency: Certain items like Hats (50%) and Sneakers (49.66%) are highly dependent on discounts for sales.

5. Business Recommendations

Boost Subscriptions: Develop exclusive benefits to convert the 73% non-subscriber base.
Loyalty Rewards: Implement programs to move "Returning" shoppers into the "Loyal" segment.
Targeted Marketing: Focus high-budget campaigns on the "Young Adult" demographic and promote "Express Shipping" to increase average order value.
